Our Rafting Trips

Half-Day Trip

A 4-hour adventure with exciting rapids and calm stretches, perfect for beginners and families. Your guide covers safety and paddling basics before you launch.

Full-Day Trip

An 8-hour journey covering more river, bigger rapids and a riverside lunch. Best for rafters who want the complete experience.

Sunset Float

A relaxed 2-hour evening float on calm water as the sun goes down. A great choice for couples, groups and anyone who wants a peaceful ride.
