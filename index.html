Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa, often called the "Giant of Africa" due to its large population — it's the most populous country in Africa, with over 200 million people. Its capital is Abuja, though Lagos is the largest city and main commercial hub. Nigeria is known for its cultural diversity, with over 250 ethnic groups (the largest being Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo) and hundreds of languages, though English is the official language.
